# Co-grown SHG tower, recursive addressing and machines: specification V2

Status: proposed integration contract, source-inspected on 2026-10-08. Supersedes V1, particularly its transfer of graph ownership to Catlab and its independently staged graph/routing adapters. This document does not claim implemented unified construction, completed acceptance, executable VM or a Git branch merge.

## 1. Objective and acceptance boundary

Use one Goggles/SemAlgebra-governed construction to grow the semantic graph, coordination, machine definitions, typed relations, boundaries, recursive addressing and bundled registries. Reuse the local SHG handoff, bounded construction/FIM experiment and SS/SR machinery as checked participants in that same derivation. Produce a recursively typed SHG whose operations, ports, hyperrelations, incidences, machines, transitions, guards, effects, bodies and obligations are individually resolvable. Grow addressed open regions through checked graph transformations. Lower only regions with sufficiently resolved semantics into digest-bound source holes. Enroll executable realizations only after their applicable admission gates pass.

The authoritative target remains the supplied semantic-hypergraph ontology: all 15 universe collections, eight transformations, seven projections, nine invariants and eight lifecycle labels. The handoff's 98-sort schema is a checked representation/projection candidate, not the semantic authority and not permission to omit ontology sections. An ontology coverage ledger must map every collection and required field to native sorts/links, explicitly unresolved obligations or a named schema extension. A textual payload is not implementation of its semantics.

First acceptance is structural synthesis, typed machine representation, one bounded interpreted machine, recursive address correspondence, checked revision and a source-bound FIM transaction. General VM execution, arbitrary effects, new-rule discovery, full self-hosting and unconditional liveness are not prerequisites. Structural acceptance must remain distinct from behavioral proof and executable activation.

## 3. One authoritative construction and successor

The SHG is semantic authority. Goggles' governed native graph construction, validation and admission path owns the proposed SHG's derivation and evolution. There is one admitted generation and one growth transaction, not independently writable Goggles, Catlab and routing graphs.

Catlab supplies a schema-driven projection, structural checks and optional rewrite proposals/witnesses. Catlab import, validation or DPO success cannot independently publish an authoritative SHG successor. A Catlab-proposed delta must be translated back to the exact native subject and admitted by Goggles under the same construction law and transaction. The 98-sort representation must preserve the required ontology without replacing the native graph with an opaque payload.

SS/SR supplies derived source headers, scope exports, route images, executable bindings and activation artifacts. These are generated consequences of the registered realization laws, tied to the same construction lineage. Neither enrollment nor activation independently changes graph semantics. An OPEN region may have structural addressing and symbolic navigation while its executable binding remains explicitly unbound.

Co-grown means one derivation contract with dependent stages. It does not require one pass, one programming language or simultaneous digest calculation. Every derived address, machine, registry row and realization must resolve to an admitted construction law application and the exact candidate generation. Post-hoc caller-authored tables attached to unrelated graphs fail acceptance.

The existing v2 experiment remains historical reference evidence. Its Python template-copying meta-construction is not the target general construction interpreter. Its narrower local SHG schema cannot silently replace the full ontology. The Catlab VM lift is supplied-topology elaboration, not a discovery engine.

Implementation ownership: native registered construction laws and the transaction gate belong in Goggles. Proposed modules are `shg_construction_algebra_v1.lisp` (typed operators/laws), `shg_coordinated_growth_v1.lisp` (call-region construction), and `shg_growth_transaction_v1.lisp` (staging, correspondence and admission). These names denote required extensions, not existing APIs. Existing owning modules should be extended instead when source inspection identifies the corresponding seam.

Cross-tool participants can be loaded through thin adapters under `fim-experiment/shg-integration-v1/`. Their only authority is the capability/contract granted by a registered native law; a host `run.py` cannot assemble topology or independently authorize a successor. Use configured source paths and exact pins, not copied runtime forks.

## 4. Shared typed algebra and complete growth transaction

### 4.1 Construction basis

Use one extensible typed construction algebra:

- `append(scope, typed-object-or-relation)`: add a lawful owned object/association with explicit identity and references.
- `nest(owner, child-graph-construction)`: build an owned graph interior while preserving definition/occurrence distinction and inherited constraints.
- `enclose(interior, typed-boundary-map)`: establish a complete external interface and checked inner correspondence.

These are proposed construction meanings, not claims that all three generic functions currently execute natively. Register them, or equivalent existing native operators, with exact admitted constitutions, operand/parameter types, effects, identity/allocation policy, laws and materializer attachments. Parameter binding is separate from boundary enclosure. `bracket` is not an undeclared fourth primitive; represent it as append of typed constraint relations only when the relevant law supports that meaning.

Typed schemas specialize this basis for ports, relations, operations, machines, transitions and rule objects. A transition and a port share construction machinery without sharing semantics. No rule may acquire authority by being stored as data.

A higher-order construction produces a complete rule object: match, requires, parameter/matched/fresh binding declarations, construction program, obligations, source pins and admission profile. Check it before registry admission. Rule generation must execute an addressed construction program; a trace naming append/nest/enclose while copying a template is insufficient. Rule-level behavioral theorems and instance structural receipts remain different scopes.

### 4.2 Native production and participants

Supply partial declarations, finite catalog, goal, permitted effects, state/resource/authority constraints, bounds and connectivity/coordination requirements. Completed output topology, generated addresses and hand-authored final route rows are not permitted inputs.

Invoke the appropriate existing native producer, retain its product, synthesis witness, control-search witness, relation evidence and theorems, and independently check their identities. Use registered construction laws to specialize/grow its native carrier into the complete SHG candidate. Do not describe the existing sparse producer as already generating every machine or every ontology field.

A registered Catlab projection maps actual native hierarchy, definitions/instances, ports, relations/ordered incidences, machines, state/effects and open obligations into supported native sorts. Shared catalogs remain read-only; preserve typed imports and boundary maps. Validate bidirectional correspondence against actual native source rows, not labels. A rejected or unsupported field cannot disappear into a Value payload. Optional rewrite checking returns a source-pinned proposal/witness to the native transaction, not a new authority.

Machine construction, boundary construction and structural registry consequences are produced by the same rule program. Address and route derivation run at their dependency-ready points in that program. Catlab/SR output is verified against the exact candidate before admission. Unsupported realization stays open with a typed obligation; it is not patched with an arbitrary route.

### 4.3 Transaction contract

Proposed `SHG_GROWTH_TRANSACTION_V1` retains:

- Transaction identity, exact selected native subject address and predecessor snapshot/generation.
- Construction program, rule/constitution/registry identities, admitted scope and exact input pins.
- Matched, parameter and fresh bindings; transaction-stable allocation namespace and ownership.
- Semantic and coordination delta, preserved-object set, boundary/effect correspondence.
- Derived H001 registry set, H002 coordinates, graph-member and registry provenance.
- Catlab correspondence/check receipts and any source-bound DPO proposal/witness.
- Derived SS/SR projection/binding rows when supported, otherwise addressed unbound slots.
- Obligation ledger, proof/check receipts, required gate policy and candidate snapshot/manifest identities.

One transaction has these dependent phases:

1. Match and pin the source; check rule applicability and typed bindings.
2. Execute the admitted construction program to stage the semantic/coordination delta.
3. Validate types, scopes, sharing, machine associations, incidences, boundaries and effect containment.
4. Close structural bundles and derive member addresses and navigation/export registries.
5. Run required Catlab and SS/SR projections through registered capability contracts; verify exact correspondence and provenance.
6. Check required receipts and recursively close the admission obligations for the requested guarantee scope.
7. Publish the complete successor through the Goggles owner using an expected-predecessor check. Retain failure/rejection evidence without changing the current graph.

Publication must not expose a new machine with old registries or a new registry with old boundaries. Durable publication/compare-and-swap is a required owner capability to implement/verify, not established by an in-memory DPO result. Derived debug files may be incomplete while staging, but cannot count as an admitted product.

Structural admission can retain open implementation obligations if the explicit structural policy allows them. Execution requires its own stronger gate. Graph construction does not activate a provider or binary merely because a realization registry exists.

## 5. Recursive hyperaddressing contract

Do not unify incompatible coordinate formats by equal names or one digest.

1. **Authoritative native coordinates:** retain complete H002 occurrence path, bundle snapshot, registry kind, row/facet/lineage and address identity. Reconstruct and resolve against the pinned native closure.
2. **Catlab projection coordinates:** use the handoff's graph generation/content/schema pins and exact entity/typed-row coordinates, bound back to the authoritative native subject. Field coordinates retain parent coordinate, schema hash, field, RFC6901 pointer and value hash. Validate through existing typed readers.
3. **Source realization coordinates:** retain SS recursive source address, parent boundary, signature, source/body/header/bundle digests and exact enrollment fields.
4. **Executable coordinates:** retain SR addressed query/scope frames, consumer/profile/traversal authority, selected export, realization and activation digest.

Define `SHG_ADDRESS_CORRESPONDENCE_V1` as a proposed integration record, not an existing upstream schema. It is derived inside the growth transaction and binds full coordinates, their exact source pins, member kind and a named correspondence law/check receipt. It cannot be authored independently after graph admission. Semantic membership correspondence may be one-to-many for declaration/instance elaboration. Only a concrete selected operation realization may acquire an executable binding; require its exact signature and admitted body.

Root, nested Graph, Body, Hole, Port, Relation, each Incidence, MachineState, Transition, Guard, Effect and proof subject must resolve independently. Relations are addressed objects, not just drawn lines. Hyperrelations whose participants include other relations must preserve that endpoint identity.

Recursive navigation follows native ownership, imports, exports and boundary maps. Literal string-path concatenation is not access permission. Resolve every intermediate boundary, reject missing/ambiguous/stale coordinates and retain the traversal witness. Root generation and nested graph generations must be separately represented; a scoped map pins the complete involved snapshot set rather than assuming every child has the same number.

PR #47's `scope::export` is the callable-facing coordinate, not a complete address of all graph members. Its fixture-local packed namespace and hash-based surface slots must retain collision rejection; this merge makes no global ID allocation claim.

Hash ordering is an acyclic dependency within the same construction: semantic candidate/base registries → bundle closure/snapshot → H002 addresses → derived correspondence/route/realization artifacts → final transaction manifest. Use the original H001 closure implementation's actual hash domains; it includes generated registry identity. Do not place snapshot-bound addresses inside the same hash input that creates that snapshot. Keep address-bearing derived artifacts in the transaction's dependent manifest unless an existing owner supplies a checked alternate construction. The complete manifest is sealed and admitted with the candidate; sequential derivation does not make it a separate authoritative graph.

## 6. Machines, typed effects and runtime state

Construct machines and their typed associations as native graph members through the shared algebra. The Catlab projection uses StateMachine/MachineState/Transition, TransitionGuard, TransitionOperation, NodeMachine and OccurrenceState links; those sorts do not acquire separate admission authority. Guard expressions must have checked operand/result types and explicit reads. Correlation schemas and state accesses resolve to actual native members.

The frozen schema's `Transition.event` is Text, not by itself a typed event reference. A proposed machine-profile correspondence must resolve each event token to an exact ValueType/contract and validation rule. Missing event typing blocks interpretation. If intrinsic typed-event links are required, propose a versioned schema extension and migrator; do not silently change the 98-sort schema.

Transition effects require exact scope. A whole-operation effect set does not prove a transition's narrower set. Represent transition actions through distinct declared operations where supported, or require an explicit checked transition-to-action/effect correspondence. The v2 zero-budget path's completion-consuming terminate effect remains an unresolved mismatch until exhaustion has a lawful input contract.

First interpreted profile is a single-flight, fuel-bounded call loop:

| Event | Preconditions | Protected update |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Issue | Ready, fuel > 0 | Reserve fresh activation; consume fuel; set pending; enter Waiting |
| Matching completion / continue | Waiting; exact generation, incarnation, invocation, iteration, attempt; typed result; fuel > 0 | Commit once, clear pending, update carried state, return Ready |
| Matching completion / stop or halt | Same correlation/type gate; applicable exit guard | Commit once, clear pending, publish permitted result, enter terminal state |
| Matching completion / trap | Same correlation/type gate; trap guard | Apply declared trap preservation policy, clear pending, terminate |
| Exhaust | Ready, fuel = 0 | Terminate without fabricated worker completion |
| Wrong/stale/duplicate observation | Any failed matching/readiness condition | Protected state unchanged; audit may record rejection |

A reusable machine definition is distinct from each machine instance and its runtime configuration. `pending` is Option<ActivationKey>: absent in Ready/terminal and present in Waiting. Each instance owns its fuel/carried state/committed set; sharing a definition never shares mutable runtime state. Activation identity includes a durable execution incarnation, pinned machine/graph generation, invocation, input generation, iteration and attempt. Never restart counters in a namespace that permits old completions to match. Immutable graph state is distinct from runtime phase/pending/commit state.

Interpret only a versioned, admitted profile. Catlab currently stores/validates machines; it is not a general machine executor. The v2 Protocol supplies a bounded reference implementation, not universal semantics for every SHG transition. A general executor must consume the graph's checked transitions, rather than maintain an unrelated Python machine decorated with graph rows.

For wait/join, require same snapshot/input generation/correlation, all required typed observations, a checked guard, duplicate handling and one accepted activation identity. Fan-out has explicit child activation identities and join membership. Missing results leave readiness false. Conditional progress or timeout semantics must be declared; finite fuel alone does not prove termination while Waiting.

State/effect edges remain distinct from call and control edges. A relation linking state owners is not a read/write implementation. Each runtime state access has ownership, consistency and commit semantics. EffectIntent/Activation/Return/Commit records are retained declarations until a verified lifecycle owner exists. The durable external effect lifecycle stays with its existing owner; do not infer exactly-once external execution from at-most-once result commit.

## 7. Construction driver and candidate retention

Register finite construction laws for function scope, argument/result binding, branch alternatives, call activation/continuation, sequence, fuel-bounded loop, async dispatch and join. Each law states typed match/parameter/fresh binding classes, allocation scope, construction program, boundary/effect preservation requirements and admission obligations.

Use SemAlgebra's operator/topology/parameter search and recursive reuse. Do not build a parallel general optimizer. The current recursive planner selects a solution, and relation-goal planning requires exactly one admissible constitution. Therefore the requested `publish without selecting interpretation` needs an explicit alternative-retention interface, not a renamed selected result. Retain candidate DAGs and rejection evidence while guaranteeing no executable selection for ambiguous candidates. If the existing planner cannot expose alternatives without selection, add a bounded owner API and verify it; do not claim current support.

Each construction transaction returns the complete jointly derived product described in section 4.3, including machine structure, address/registry closure and provenance; a graph-only return followed by manually added routing is incomplete. Rejected candidates remain evidence; context-required candidates suspend with named missing inputs. Rejection must consume attempt budget.

Initial integration limits: depth 8, 128 occurrences, 512 relations, 4096 added native records per transaction, 16 candidates per goal and 256 expanded goals. Loop demonstration fuel is 0..2; retry count is zero; live provider count is zero. These are proposed limits checked by the driver, not claimed existing producer options. The sparse wrapper does not expose the planner's budget keyword: verify defaults or add explicit owner budget plumbing before claiming these limits govern native search.

## 8. Lowering, FIM and executable enrollment

Resolved semantics lower progressively: coarse structure → typed calls and effects → operation/state/control graph → source AST/signatures → exact source span → candidate body. Unknown VM widths, encodings, traps, aliasing or effect order remain holes. No FIM request may choose those semantics implicitly.

Reuse handoff typed-lowering contracts and native compiler owners with explicit correspondence. Existing protocol→AFSM→compiler IR and CFG/SynthSlotPlan/IR1 functions do not establish automatic compatibility with every SHG product. Preserve the distinct direct CompilerIR/SynthSlotPlan route and bounded IR2/CFG route.

A FIM request binds graph/schema pins, exact OperationInstance/Body/Hole coordinates, source digest, UTF-8 half-open byte range and preimage, AST role, helpers/types/contracts, permitted effects and verification policy. Prefix/suffix come from the exact current source. Model input is code context; graph ownership and admission remain tool-side. Recheck preimage before insertion and preserve every byte outside the authorized span.

Compile/test/prove candidate as required. Passing finite tests does not mark semantic obligations PROVEN. Propose the candidate through the same Goggles growth/admission transaction with native source/body correspondence; project BodyCode/mutation checks into Catlab where supported. DEFINED and VALIDATED remain distinct. Recompute affected dependencies and address maps. FIM realization is a downstream projection of the retained graph.

PR #47 currently admits only `(x: i64) -> i64` native callables, no nonlocal captures or arbitrary effects. Reproduce its three-function slice unchanged first. A separate typed callable ABI extension is required before enrolling stateful VM or string-returning classify/print functions. Do not fake compatibility with casts or erased JSON.

The registered realization law derives source/header/export/realization bindings with the existing SS/SR producers as dependent transaction products, and verifies their correspondence before the requested admission gate. `prepare` must preserve active code; `activate` must require expected predecessor, artifact hashes and closed required gates. Host activation atomicity is not a proof that arbitrary machine transitions or external effects commit atomically.

## 9. Proofs, receipts and closure

Reuse original Goggles proof checking, deterministic rechecking, formal admission and selective invalidation. Every receipt binds exact subject coordinate, source/target pins, rule/registry, proposition, assumptions, verification method/version, domain/bounds, dependency receipts and artifact hashes.

Separate these gates:

- Structural: native typing, scopes, identities, ordered incidences, references, boundaries and complete correspondence.
- Machine safety: pending-phase consistency, no identity reuse, at-most-once result commit, rejected-observation immutability, declared state/effect updates.
- Behavioral preservation: predecessor/candidate and graph/implementation correspondence for the actual contract.
- Liveness: progress under explicit fairness/completion assumptions, or a verified timeout/cancellation policy.
- Executable: all blocking claims plus actual callable/router/runtime compatibility and activation integrity.

The v2 rank theorem is reusable only for its exact retained table arithmetic proposition, or after checked correspondence derives a new table. Kani tests are external bounded evidence until an independently checked native import/correspondence exists. Presence of an artifact ID or a generic receipt cannot establish the proposition's semantic correspondence.

Closure rejects open/refuted/unknown/stale/wrong-scope blocking obligations and recursively validates dependencies. Assumptions stay visible. Timeout is UNKNOWN; missing tool is NOT_RUN. Type/rule/source changes invalidate dependent current applicability; historical receipts remain valid for their historical subjects. Reuse unaffected evidence only through a checked frame/dependency correspondence.
